## 1. Project Introduction & Business Context

**FUJI SMART DX** is an enterprise-level smart tourism and transportation management system built around the business operations of **Fuji Kyuko Co., Ltd. (富士急行株式会社)**, Japan. 

Fuji Kyuko operates integrated railways (the Fujikyuko Line), extensive bus networks, Mount Fuji climbing trailheads, amusement facilities (Fuji-Q Highland), resort hotels, and tourism cruise vessels across the Yamanashi and Mount Fuji region.

This platform demonstrates how machine learning, real data engineering, and automation can unify fragmented operations, accurately forecast visitor flows, balance railway and bus capacity, and provide multilingual AI guidance to international tourists visiting Mount Fuji.

## 2. Real-Data Principle & Architectural Integrity

The system enforces strict real-data integrity rules:
1. **Never generates fake or randomized business data.**
2. **Every data point has a traceable source, collection date range, and last-updated timestamp.**
3. **Data Provenance Categories:**
   - 🟢 **Live Data**: Retrieved directly from external APIs (e.g., Japan Meteorological Agency live weather feeds).
   - 🔵 **Historical Data**: Official statistical reports (e.g., Yamanashi Tourism Survey, JNTO, Fujikyuko Line Passenger logs).
   - 🟣 **Uploaded Data**: Provided by authorized administrators via validated CSV/XLSX/JSON pipelines.
   - 💠 **Derived Analytics**: Calculated strictly from underlying database records (e.g., occupancy rates, daily averages).
   - 🟡 **ML Predictions**: Generated by models genuinely trained and evaluated on historical time-series data with published MAE/RMSE/MAPE residual metrics.
   - ⚪ **Unavailable Data**: Honestly displayed empty states with connection guides if external integrations are missing.

## 3. Technology Stack

- **React.js 19** + **TypeScript**

### Backend
- **Python 3.11+ / 3.13**
- **FastAPI** + **Uvicorn**
- **Pydantic v2** & **Pydantic-Settings**
- **SQLAlchemy 2.1** ORM
- **Psycopg 3.3** PostgreSQL driver
- **Pandas 3.1** & **NumPy 2.5**
- **PyJWT & Bcrypt** (Role-Based Access Control)

### Database
- **PostgreSQL 18** (`localhost:5432/fuji_smart_dx`)

### AI & Machine Learning
- **Scikit-learn 1.9.1** (Random Forest Regressor, Ridge Regression)
- **Chronological train/test split (70% Train / 30% Test)** to prevent data leakage
- **Evaluation Metrics**: Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE), Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE)
- **Joblib** for model serialization & persistent registry

## 4. 13 Functional Modules

1. **Overview Dashboard**: Executive KPIs, total visits, ridership, occupancy, revenue, period filters (Today, 7d, 30d, 90d).
2. **Tourism Analytics**: Mount Fuji 5th Station, Kawaguchiko, Fuji-Q, Chureito Pagoda, Oshino Hakkai, Yamanakako seasonality & international tourist origin breakdown.
3. **Transportation Analytics**: 18 stations on the Fujikyuko Line (Otsuki FJ01 to Kawaguchiko FJ18), bus routes, and verified published timetables.
4. **Demand Forecasting**: 7-day and 30-day horizons with 90% empirical prediction intervals.
5. **Hotel Analytics**: Fuji Kyuko Hotel Group properties (Highland Resort, Fuji View Hotel, etc.), occupancy calculations `(Occupied / Available) × 100`.
6. **Booking Management**: Validated reservation ledger, duplicate checking, travel dates, status, and financial tallies.
7. **AI Tourism Assistant**: Multilingual retrieval engine (English, Japanese, Hindi) providing verified responses on trains, park passes, trails, and live weather.
8. **Route Intelligence**: OpenStreetMap Leaflet GIS map with railway stations, sightseeing hubs, and verified itineraries.
9. **Data Sources**: Live connector health, JMA test sync, OSM status, and ingestion logs.
10. **Data Upload & Validation**: CSV, XLSX, and JSON drag-and-drop with column type detection, null counts, duplicate detection, and PostgreSQL ingestion.
11. **ML Model Management**: Algorithm registry, chronological evaluation metrics, and retrain triggers.
12. **Business Reports**: Daily, weekly, and monthly reports with CSV download and metric summaries.
13. **System Settings**: Database connection status, JWT RBAC settings, and API configuration.
